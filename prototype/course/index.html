Gradient Descent

How a model walks downhill on its loss, one step at a time.

Started
Needs: Loss Function · Derivatives · by Ziming's Lab
Step 1 · Predict first

The learning rate is how big each step is. What happens if we make it very large, say 2.1?

Make a prediction to unlock the experiment
Step 2 · Experiment

Try 0.1, 1.0 and 2.1. Watch the dot and the loss counter.

Step 3 · Why it works

The slope tells you which way is downhill. Gradient descent moves the weight a little in that direction, then checks the slope again.

w ← w − η · dL/dw

The learning rate η sets the step size. Too small and you crawl. Too large and every step overshoots the bottom, landing higher on the other side, until the loss explodes. For this bowl, any η above 2 diverges.

Step 4 · Challenge

Q1. Your training loss jumps around and then becomes NaN. What should you try first?

Q2. Lab challenge: get the loss below 0.01 in 5 steps or fewer.

Use the experiment above. Reset, pick a learning rate, and step.
